The problem

Most breaches start at a firewall nobody's watched since install day

Default configurations, unpatched firmware, and open ports left over from a one-time setup are how attackers get in — not sophisticated exploits.

  • Firewall rules never reviewed after the initial install
  • No VPN, so remote staff use unsecured connections
  • No one gets alerted when firmware falls out of date

FAQ

Firewall & security questions

Which firewall brands does KroxIT install?

We work with Fortinet FortiGate, SonicWall, pfSense and Sophos, and pick the platform based on your traffic, budget, and existing infrastructure rather than pushing one brand.

Can you set up VPN access for remote employees?

Yes — we configure both site-to-site VPNs between office locations and remote-access VPNs for staff working from home, with multi-factor authentication where supported.

We already have a firewall — can you just manage it?

Yes. We regularly take over management of existing firewalls: auditing the current rule set, closing gaps, and bringing it onto our AMC for ongoing patching and monitoring.

How long does a typical installation take?

A single-office firewall install with VPN configuration is usually completed in a day. Multi-site or high-availability setups are scoped after a network assessment.

Do you offer ongoing monitoring after installation?

Yes — AMC clients get firmware patching, rule reviews, and alerting on suspicious traffic as part of their contract, not just a one-time install.
