FAQ

CCTV & surveillance questions

Can I view my CCTV cameras from my phone?

Yes — every installation includes a remote viewing app so you can check any camera from your phone, anywhere with an internet connection.

Do you offer cloud storage for footage?

Yes, alongside local NVR storage we can set up cloud backup so footage survives even if the on-site recorder is damaged or stolen.

How many cameras do I need for my office?

It depends on entry points, floor area, and blind spots — we do a free on-site survey and recommend camera count and placement before quoting.

Can CCTV be integrated with biometric access control?

Yes, we regularly install both together so door events and camera footage sit on one dashboard for easier review.

What warranty do you offer on installations?

Hardware carries the manufacturer's warranty, and our installation workmanship is covered under our own service warranty — details are confirmed in your quote.
