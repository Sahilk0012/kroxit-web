FAQ

Biometric access control questions

Can biometric attendance integrate with our payroll software?

Most systems we install export attendance data in formats compatible with common payroll software, or we can configure direct integration where supported.

What happens to access control during a power outage?

We fit devices with a battery backup so doors keep working through short outages, and can add mechanical override for longer ones.

Can we combine fingerprint and face recognition on one device?

Yes, several device lines support both methods on a single unit, useful when hygiene or speed requirements vary by entry point.

Do you support multi-branch or multi-floor businesses?

Yes — devices across sites report into one cloud dashboard so you can see attendance and access logs for every location in one place.

What warranty comes with biometric devices?

Devices carry the manufacturer's warranty, and our installation and configuration work is covered under our own service warranty.
