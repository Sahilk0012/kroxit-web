FAQ

Microsoft 365 questions

Can you migrate us from Google Workspace or an old email host?

Yes — we plan and run mailbox and file migrations from Google Workspace or other hosts to Microsoft 365 with minimal downtime, usually scheduled outside business hours.

Do you set up multi-factor authentication?

Yes, MFA and a security baseline are part of every tenant setup — it's the single biggest step against account takeover.

What about backup for Microsoft 365 data?

Microsoft 365's built-in retention isn't a full backup. We can configure a proper backup for mail, SharePoint and OneDrive so deleted or corrupted data is always recoverable.

Can you help us reduce our licensing costs?

Yes — a licensing review is part of our assessment. We regularly find businesses paying for tiers or seats they aren't using.

Do you provide training for our team?

Yes, a short handover session on Teams, SharePoint and the security basics is included so your team isn't left guessing after go-live.
