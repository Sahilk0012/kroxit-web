FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I get my device repaired?

Book a consultation or message us on WhatsApp with the issue, and our certified engineers will take it from there — including diagnostics, a transparent quote, and genuine-parts repair with warranty.

How fast is emergency support?

AMC clients get guaranteed onsite response under signed SLAs — as fast as 2 hours on the Enterprise plan. Non-contract requests are handled on a best-effort priority basis.

Can you handle multi-building networks?

Absolutely. We design fiber backbones, structured cabling, VLAN segmentation, and high-density mesh Wi-Fi for multi-storey and multi-site commercial setups.

How do I get AMC pricing?

Pick a plan on the AMC page and message us on WhatsApp with your site details, and we'll get back with accurate pricing after a free site survey.

Do repairs come with a warranty?

Yes — we use genuine parts and back every repair with a warranty. You'll get a transparent diagnostic and quote before any work begins.
